Delete your UpYou account

Last updated: 2026-11-24

This page explains how to delete your account in UpYou, the goal-tracking app by Nataliia Maksymiuk, and what happens to your data.

An account in UpYou is optional. If you never signed in, there is no account to delete: all your data is on your device, and uninstalling the App removes it.

Delete your account in the App

  1. Open UpYou.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Ask us to delete your account

If you can no longer open the App (for example, you uninstalled it or lost your phone), email nataliia.apps@gmail.com from the email address of the Apple or Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my UpYou account". If you used Apple's "Hide My Email", tell us that you signed in with Apple and we will help you confirm the account. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (identifier, email address, name, and the link to your Apple or Google sign-in)
  • Your cloud backup: all goals, progress, check-ins, values, journal entries, reflections, and "About you" details stored in the cloud
  • If you signed in with Apple, the App's access to your Apple ID is revoked

What is kept

  • Data on your device. Goals stay on the phone until you delete them or uninstall the App. To remove them, uninstall UpYou.
  • Gift records. If you bought or redeemed a gift code, the gift record is kept as a record of the purchase, but it is no longer linked to your account. A gift you bought and that was not redeemed yet stays valid for its recipient.
  • Purchase records at Apple, Google, and RevenueCat (including the email and name linked to your purchases at RevenueCat), as required for tax and accounting, according to their policies. You can ask us to remove your email and name from these records by writing to the address below.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. After you delete your account, Premium linked to it is no longer available in the App.
