Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-22
Last updated: 2026-11-23

This Privacy Policy explains how Nataliia Maksymiuk ("Nataliia", "we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the UpYou mobile application ("App"). It applies to all users of the App on iOS and Android.

UpYou is local-first: your goals and progress are stored on your device and the App works fully without an account. If you choose to sign in, we keep a backup copy of your App data in the cloud so you can restore it on another device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored on your device

When you use UpYou, the following information is created and stored on your device, in a local database:

  • Goals you create (title, description, category, status, dates, tracking method, reminders, progress)
  • Your check-in history (done / not done days, streaks) and the values you log toward target-value goals
  • Journal entries (optional): the text you write in journal goals, and the question you set for each one. Entries can contain very personal thoughts (for example about your mood or wellbeing). We do not read, analyse or use them for anything except storing them on your device and, if you have an account, in your backup. Unsaved drafts stay on your device only and are not backed up.
  • Categories you create
  • Your answers to the App's New Year reflection ("Your year")
  • About you (optional): the name you want to be addressed by and your birthday (day and month; the year is optional)
  • App settings (language, theme, notification preferences) and your Premium status (cached so the App knows which features to show)

Without an account, this information does not leave your device unless you export it yourself (see Section 5).

1.2 If you create an account (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or Google. Accounts are provided through Supabase (our backend provider; data is hosted in the European Union, Frankfurt). When you sign in we process:

  • Account details shared by Apple or Google: an account identifier, your email address (with Apple this may be a private relay address if you chose "Hide My Email"), your name if the provider shares it, and, from Google, a link to your profile picture.
  • Cloud backup: a copy of all your App data listed in Section 1.1 — including your goals, progress, journal entries, reflections, name and birthday — together with the time of the last backup, the number of goals, your platform (iOS / Android) and the App version. The backup is updated automatically a few seconds after you make changes, and is used to restore your data on a new or reinstalled device.

Access rules on the server let only your signed-in account read or change your backup.

1.3 Gift Premium

If you buy Premium as a gift, we store the gift code, the gift length, the store transaction identifier, the sender name and personal message you write, the card design, and whether and when the gift was redeemed and by which account. The recipient sees the sender name and message. Both the buyer and the recipient need an account.

1.4 Information processed by third parties

  • Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android): distribute the App, provide Sign in with Apple / Google, and process in-app purchases under their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat: manages and validates purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat processes an app-user ID, the products you purchased, purchase receipts, country, app version and platform. If you are signed in, RevenueCat also receives your account identifier, email address and name, so that your Premium follows your account across devices and we can help you with purchase questions. RevenueCat does not receive your goals or other App content.
  • Supabase: hosts accounts, cloud backups and gift codes (see Sections 1.2 and 1.3).

We do not receive your payment card details, Apple ID password, or Google account password.

1.5 Information we do not collect

  • Your location, photos, contacts, calendar, or microphone data
  • Usage analytics, behavioral data, crash reports or telemetry
  • Advertising identifiers (we show no ads and use no ad SDKs)

We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, Crashlytics, or any similar analytics or tracking service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Provide the App's features on your device (including reminders and the birthday greeting)
  • Keep and restore your cloud backup, if you have an account
  • Process and validate purchases and subscriptions, restore them on other devices, and handle refunds and renewals
  • Create and redeem gift codes
  • Answer your support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax records held by Apple / Google)

We do not use any information to profile you, target ads, or train machine-learning models.

3. Notifications

UpYou uses local notifications (goal reminders, check-in and journal reminders, streak milestones, the New Year reflection and, if you added your birthday, a birthday greeting). They are scheduled and delivered on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server. You can turn notifications off in the App's Settings or in your device's settings.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named in Section 1.4, and only for the purposes in Section 2. We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not sell or rent personal information.

5. Your Data, Your Control

  • Export / import. Settings → Import / Export creates a file with all your data on your device, or restores one.
  • Edit "About you". Change or clear your name and birthday at any time in Settings → About you.
  • Delete content. You can delete individual goals and categories. Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on that device.
  • Sign out. Signing out stops the cloud backup on that device; your data stays on the device.
  • Delete your account. In Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and your cloud backup from our servers. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. Instructions, including how to ask us to delete your account if you no longer have the App: https://nataliia-apps.github.io/upyou-legal/delete-account.html

You can also contact us (Section 12) to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you.

6. Children

UpYou is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us.

7. Data Retention

  • On your device: until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Account and cloud backup: until you delete your account. Only the latest backup is kept; each backup replaces the previous one.
  • Gift codes: kept as a record of the purchase. If the buyer or recipient deletes their account, the gift record stays but is no longer linked to that account.
  • Apple / Google / RevenueCat: according to their own policies and applicable tax and accounting law.

8. Security

Data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and server access rules limit each account to its own data. On your device, we rely on your operating system's security; use a passcode or biometric lock. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. International Users

Accounts, backups and gift codes are hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Purchase information may be processed by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat in other countries, including the United States, under their own privacy frameworks and standard contractual clauses where required.

9.1 If you are in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland

Under the GDPR / UK GDPR, you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Our lawful bases are:

  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — to provide the App, your account, cloud backup, purchases and gifts
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for the optional "About you" details; you can remove them at any time
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — to prevent fraud (for example, guessing gift codes) and keep the App secure
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) — for tax and accounting

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9.2 If you are in California (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information as defined under the CCPA/CPRA. You have the right to know what personal information we have, to request its deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.
