UpYou Support

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Thank you for using UpYou — a goal tracker that helps you set goals, make progress and grow.

If you have a question, found a bug, or have an idea, write to us at nataliia.apps@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. It helps if you mention your phone model, iOS or Android version, and the UpYou version (Settings → Version).

Goals and tracking

  • Four ways to track a goal. Progress bar — you update the percentage yourself. Check-ins — mark each planned day done; several reminders a day mean several check-ins (for example, drinking water 5 times a day). Target value — log a number toward a result (for example, 80 → 70 kg). Journal — write a few lines on your reminder days: a gratitude journal, a mood diary or notes for therapy.
  • Journal entries. An entry on a reminder day counts for that day. Entries on other days, and entries written ahead for a future date, are notes: a future day counts once you add to or edit its entry on the day itself.
  • No end date? A goal that never ends has no finish line, so its ring shows the number of days you've done instead of a percentage.
  • Ideas. Turn off Start right away when creating a goal to save it as an idea: no reminders, not on Today. Tap Start when you're ready.
  • Forgot to mark a day? Anything left unmarked from the past 7 days waits on Today under Left from previous days. Mark it done, or tap ✕ if you didn't do it. Days in this window don't break your streak.
  • Free plan. Up to 3 goals of any status (archived and completed goals count too). Delete a goal to free a slot, or get Premium for unlimited goals.

Reminders

  • Check that notifications are allowed for UpYou in your phone's settings and turned on in Settings in the app.
  • Android: reminders can be late or missing because of battery saving. Open Settings → On-time reminders in UpYou and follow the steps (allow exact alarms, turn off battery optimisation for UpYou).
  • On iPhone, Focus modes can hide notifications.

Account and backup

  • An account is optional. Sign in with Apple or Google in Settings → Account to back up your data to the cloud automatically.
  • New phone: install UpYou and sign in with the same account — your goals come back on their own.
  • Without an account: use Settings → Import / Export to save a file and open it on the new phone.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. More details: Delete your account.

Premium and subscriptions

  • Restore purchases: open the Premium screen and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If you're signed in to UpYou, Premium also follows your account.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your store account:
  • iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → UpYou.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → UpYou.
  • Uninstalling the App or deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google: Apple, Google Play.

Gift Premium

  • Give: Settings → Gifts → Gift Premium — choose one year or lifetime, add a card and a message, and send the code.
  • Redeem: Settings → Gifts → I have a gift code (you need to be signed in).
  • A code works once. If something went wrong with a gift, write to us with the code.
